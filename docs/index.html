WHAT IT IS

An assistant that acts, not just chats

Most AI assistants end at the chat window. JARVIS starts there and keeps going — into your apps, your reminders, your notes and your browser.

⚡

Instant where it counts

Over 140 local skills answer common commands in well under a second — no round trip, no token cost, no key required. The AI layer handles what skills don't cover.

🏠

Local-first by design

Your memory, notes, history and keys stay on your PC in SQLite and .env. Online features are optional and always something you configure yourself.

🩺

Honest about its health

One status pill tells the truth — READY, ATTENTION or SAFE MODE — from a live audit of every subsystem. When something degrades, it says so and names the fix.

WHY JARVIS

Skills first. AI second.

The order matters. Common commands are code — instant, offline, free. The model handles everything else, with tools.

STEP 3 · AI LAYER

The model fills gaps

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: chat, reasoning, 30 built-in tools.

Built to keep working when things fail

A broken skill loads isolated — it can't take down the boot. A dead model falls back down a chain. No API key? You get a working skills-only mode with 100+ commands, not an error screen. And the status pill reflects all of it, honestly, in real time.

GET STARTED

Install in 3 steps

Windows 10/11. Existing files are never touched.

Run the one-liner

Paste into PowerShell or cmd — it clones, builds the environment and runs an 11-point health check.

powershell -c "iex (irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Youssef-Devolopment/jarvis/main/setup.ps1)"

Add your AI key (optional)

Start JARVIS once, open Settings → GENERAL, paste your DEEPSEEK_API_KEY — any OpenAI-compatible provider works, verified live. Skip it entirely and you're already in skills-only mode.

Launch and talk

Double-click desktop.bat — tray icon, hotkeys and the Alt+Space HUD. Say "open notepad", "what time is it", "remind me to stretch in 20 minutes".

Prefer your hands on every step? install.bat · copy .env.example .env · start.bat — same result.
